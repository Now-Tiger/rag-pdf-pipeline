#### Original text
习近平在第四届中国国际进口博览会开幕式上发表主旨演讲

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation

Chinese President Xi Jinping delivered a keynote speech at the opening ceremony of the Fourth China International Import Expo (CIIE) on the evening of November 4, via video link.

In his speech titled “Let the Spring Breeze of Openness Warm the World”, Xi expressed his pleasure in meeting all guests and friends—both old and new—at this year’s Import Expo.

He noted that China has always honored its promises and implemented the opening-up measures announced at the previous Expo. Despite the impact of COVID-19, China promoted innovative development in foreign trade and was the only major economy to achieve positive growth in goods trade last year, making an important contribution to global industrial and supply chain stability and the recovery of the world economy.

Xi stressed that economic globalization is facing headwinds, but “to sail against the current, one must forge ahead or be left behind.” He called for supporting all countries in expanding openness, opposing unilateralism and protectionism, and working together for a better shared future for humanity.

He further emphasized that openness is a defining feature of contemporary China. This year marks the 20th anniversary of China’s accession to the World Trade Organization (WTO). Over the past two decades, China has fully fulfilled its WTO commitments, continuously expanded its openness, and infused vitality into both China’s and the world’s economies.

These 20 years have been a period of deepened reform, broad opening, and global engagement, during which China has seized opportunities, met challenges, taken responsibility, and contributed to global well-being.

Xi concluded by expressing heartfelt gratitude to all people at home and abroad who have participated in, witnessed, and supported China’s journey of opening-up and development.
